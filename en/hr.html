Details that support your day.

Recruitment and applicant tracking — ATS

Recruitment management, job description generation, job ad writing and interview planning with tailored questions and tests, assisted by AI within Enterprise.

Employee profiles and shifts

Employee management, work shifts and 360° employee profiles, with basic reports within Starter.

Attendance, leave and work locations

Mobile attendance using face or fingerprint verification, automatic location checks, multiple geofenced sites, biometric device integration and leave management within Starter, subject to device compatibility and configuration.

Tasks and team progress

Task management and team progress tracking within Pro, with role-based permissions.

Performance indicators

Measure employee results and track performance indicators within Business to understand team progress.

Payroll and daily operations

Payroll and overtime, permissions and business assignments, advances and loans with automatic installments, and violations and penalties within Pro.

Documents and organizational structure

Employee document archiving, organizational structure, allowances, salary certificates and employment letters within Business.

Company policies and intelligent assistance

An intelligent assistant for employee inquiries within Pro, plus policies and SOP management with intelligent chat within Business.

Reporting and analysis

Advanced dashboards and PDF/Excel reports within Business, plus workforce planning and gap analysis within Enterprise.

Support and account management

A dedicated account manager and around-the-clock support within Enterprise, according to the agreed service scope.

Features vary by plan: Pro includes Starter, Business includes Pro, and Enterprise includes Business. We review activation scope, compatibility and payroll policies during your demo.
